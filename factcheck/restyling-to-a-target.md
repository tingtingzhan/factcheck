
# Restyling to a target skill — borrowing a voice

Fires when the user supplies text and names a **skill whose voice it should take**. The supplied text is the record: every claim in the output traces to it, exactly as an analysis traces to a source. What you borrow is expression.

>[!important]- Read the Target Before Imitating It
>
>Locate the named skill's `SKILL.md` — the `available_skills` list in context first, then `/mnt/skills/{public,user,examples,private}/<name>/SKILL.md` — and read it. A voice you have not read cannot be matched. Two plausible matches, or none: ask which, before rewriting. That question is the one thing permitted beside the deliverable ([[#^deliverable-alone]]). ^read-the-target

>[!caution]- Style Signature Is How It Is Written, Not What It Is About
>
>Extract from the target: register (imperative, explanatory, terse, formal); person and stance; sentence rhythm; formatting habits (headers, callouts, numbered steps, bold, inline examples); characteristic openings, closings, recurring phrasings. The target's domain nouns surfacing in your output is the tell that you copied content, not voice. Take only the habits the content calls for — structure the input does not need is an empty form ([[factcheck/maintaining-this-skill]]). The bar: a reader who knows the target recognizes the voice. ^style-signature

>[!important]- Voice Transfer May Not Change a Claim's Strength
>
>A target's register pulls on the claims passing through it: a brisk imperative voice promotes *may help* to *does*; a confident one adds elevation (*isn't just $A$ — it's $B$*); one fond of examples invites you to invent one; a hedged one demotes what the input asserted outright. Each is a factual edit wearing a stylistic one. Carry every hedge, quantifier, attribution, and named thing at its input strength ([[factcheck/evidence-and-opinion#^label-fidelity]]); no example, figure, or claim absent from the input enters the output. Refining is surface work — grammar, wordiness, redundancy, word choice, flow; compression runs under [[SKILL]]'s tightest-version rule, both passes. ^strength-preserved

>[!important]- The Deliverable Prints Alone
>
>Print the restyled text and stop — no preamble, no changelog, no *"I adjusted the tone to…"*, no closing offer. The user copies the result straight out; commentary is text they delete, and explaining the edits defeats the request. Markdown source goes in a fenced block per [[SKILL]]'s output medium. Exactly two things may sit outside that block: [[#^read-the-target]]'s question, and the Pass-2 report [[SKILL]] requires — one line, after the deliverable, never inside it. ^deliverable-alone

>[!caution]- Restyling Borrows a Voice, Not a Language
>
>The supplied text keeps the language it arrived in: it is the record, and [[SKILL]]'s imperative lens sets the language of the reply, not of the record carried inside it.
